# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, no framework or build step (user-pinned). Hosted on Netlify or GitHub Pages. Files: `index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `images/`.

## Users

Primary: El Paso locals on their phones who have a broken part to replace, a gift or figurine idea, or a one-off object they want made. Many have no 3D file and no 3D printing vocabulary. Secondary: local businesses and makers who need prototypes, signs, or bulk runs. The audience is bilingual; Spanish speakers must get the full site in Spanish.

## Product Purpose

A single-page site for El Paso 3D Printing, a local, owner-run 3D printing service. Success is a quote request (form with file upload), a phone call, or an email.

## Positioning

A real person in El Paso prints your thing and you pick it up across town: local pickup at 7534 Hacienda Ave, El Paso, TX 79915, help for people who only have an idea or a photo (no 3D file), and small one-off jobs taken as seriously as bulk orders.

## Operating Context

Customers find the site on a phone, often from a search like "3D printing El Paso" or a shared link. They send an STL/OBJ file, a photo, or a description, get a quote, the owner prints it, and they pick it up or get it delivered.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: custom prints, prototypes, replacement parts, figurines, signs, bulk orders.
- Materials: PLA, PETG, TPU, resin.
- Contact: (915) 301-2296, elpaso3dprintingcompany@gmail.com.
- Full EN/ES language toggle across the whole page.
- Quote form: name, email/phone, description, file upload (STL/OBJ/photo), quantity, deadline. Form backend not yet connected.
- Undecided, shown as clear placeholders: hours, service area, social links, pricing, turnaround time, About story.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "El Paso 3D Printing". No logo yet.
- Imagery: only the owner's photos in `images/`. No stock photos.
- Dark background with one vivid, high-contrast accent; desert tones as a secondary accent; a subtle Franklin Mountains reference (user-pinned).
- Must not feel sci-fi or gimmicky (neon glows, fake HUD effects) or crafty/cute (Etsy softness).

## Evidence on Hand

- Five print photos in `images/`: a large castle print on a printer bed, white textured bud vases with carnations, a white wave-form pen holder, a white tool and screwdriver organizer, a ribbed wood-tone planter with an aloe.
- No testimonials, reviews, customer names, prices, turnaround times, or production stats exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. An idea is enough: never make a visitor feel they need a 3D file or technical words to ask.
2. Local and human over polished and corporate.
3. The prints are the proof; show them big and let them carry the page.
4. Every path ends in an easy way to ask: form, call, or email.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first. Full Spanish parity. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Alt text on every image in both languages.
