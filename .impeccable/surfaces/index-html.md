# Surface brief: index.html (single-page site)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: El Paso locals on phones first, businesses second; full EN/ES parity. Action: quote request (form with file upload), call, or email. Proof: the owner's five print photos only. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no stock, no invented prices, reviews, or turnaround; reduced motion respected; not sci-fi, not crafty. History: the first build used "Shop Drawing"; the user rejected it and "Franklin Contours" and asked for something similar to the build, then picked "Spool Label" from a safer re-roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the shop's filament spool label: a bold maker label with a swatch band, giant material codes, and spec rows, so what a local customer needs reads like the specs on a spool. It refuses the generic print-shop page (photo hero over equal cards) and the old drawing linework.

OWN-WORLD: Deep teal ground. Pale label stock with dark teal ink for the material labels and the quote form. Coral is the swatch: actions, and full-bleed bands whose top edge is the Franklin ridge. Mint for keys and annotations. The Star on the Mountain, a dotted outline of lights, sits on Ranger Peak in both bands. Condensed 800 uppercase for codes and headlines; small-caps keys over tabular values in spec rows. Labels are die-cut: 8px corners, solid fills, hairline spec rules, a fine-print row with lot 79915.

STORY: The visitor sees real prints, learns an idea or photo is enough, sees what it costs (placeholders), and sends a quote, calls, or emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: header strip (stacked name, EN | ES, Quote, menu). Three-line headline at about 58px, lede, orange Get a Quote and outlined See Our Work. Then the castle print inside a pale label (top band: name + LOT 79915; foot: sample name + layer counter). Below, the coral swatch band rising as the Franklin ridge, carrying four spec cells. Desktop: headline left, label right, band full width.

FORM: Spool Label, safer re-roll round 1, grounded candidate picked by the user, seed:792163403e531849. Signature interaction: the hero photo prints layer by layer inside the label while the layer counter on its foot climbs. Reduced motion shows it finished.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
